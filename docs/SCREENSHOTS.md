# Product screenshots

The case-study images are framed 1440×900 browser captures of the public, read-only synthetic demo. All names, teams, contacts, scenarios and ratings are invented; no production workspace was captured. They were generated by [hosted browser verification run 36509684361](https://github.com/samhillalston-sha/Blueprint-Recruitment/actions/runs/36509684361) from commit `2380ee2fc32a631d1405cc388275cec7ee227e33`. The static demo has no private backend or write endpoint.

## Case-study views

### Recruiting dashboard

Selected-season stage counts and overdue/upcoming follow-up queues. These are fictional sample totals, not production recruiting counts.

![Synthetic demo dashboard with prospect follow-up queues](images/demo-dashboard.png)

### Prospect database

Fictional names, locations, stages, owners, next actions and follow-up dates at a glance.

![Synthetic prospect database with fictional people](images/demo-prospects.png)

### Evaluation comparison

Individual invented ratings and per-attribute means show how N/A values are excluded from their own attribute's denominator.

![Fictional ratings, attribute averages and evaluator comparison](images/demo-evaluations.png)

### Historical season

One fictional person's 2026 recruiting workflow and separate outcome, shown after switching away from the active 2027 season.

![Fictional historical workflow and separate season outcome](images/demo-history.png)

### Recruiting dashboard

Selected-season counts, follow-up queues, missing owners/actions and scoped recent activity. These are synthetic test scenarios, not production recruiting totals.

![Blueprint dashboard with synthetic follow-ups and activity](images/dashboard-desktop.png)

### Evaluator comparison

Individual fictional ratings and per-attribute means demonstrate N/A exclusion and an editable own evaluation. The comparison scrolls within its container on mobile.

![Synthetic evaluation entry, averages and evaluator comparison](images/evaluations-desktop.png)

### Seasonal continuity

A fresh 2028 membership preserves the same person's 2027 result and 2026 historical record. Current seasonal fields and evaluations begin empty.

![New-season synthetic profile with previous-year outcome and preserved history](images/season-continuity-desktop.png)

### Season controls on mobile

The active season has exact-year closure confirmation. A newly closed season records its author/time; the earlier closed season retains unknown closure metadata. Starting a new season is explicit.

![Mobile settings with synthetic active and closed seasons](images/season-settings-mobile.png)
